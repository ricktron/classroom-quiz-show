## Candidate reusable workflow

For a future product surface heading toward MVP:

1. Define the **user North Star journey**.
2. Build a **journey/control-effect matrix**.
3. Classify **evidence types** before testing.
4. Prove consequential controls semantically.
5. Prove contextual detours **and return**.
6. Add an authentic **golden path early**.
7. Add branch/failure qualification.
8. Qualify the real deployment/integration environment.
9. Run independent adversarial review.
10. Issue a pre-owner eligibility verdict.
11. Let the owner perform a natural usability playthrough.
12. Harvest owner feedback as polish/design input, not first-line functional QA.
13. Journal meaningful escapes, owner friction, and preventive controls throughout.
14. After MVP, synthesize repeated lessons into the next reusable template.

## Promotion rule

A candidate should graduate into a generic reusable playbook only when:

- it is supported by concrete evidence;
- it is not merely CQS-specific architecture;
- applying it would plausibly reduce future defects or owner burden;
- its costs and failure modes are understood;
- it does not create ceremony without a corresponding risk reduction.
